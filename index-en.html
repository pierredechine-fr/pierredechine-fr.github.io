Frequently Asked Questions

Are marble tables durable for everyday use?

Yes. High-quality marble tables are highly durable and can last for decades with proper care. Marble is naturally heat-resistant and strong, making it an excellent choice for dining tables, coffee tables, and luxury interiors.

Does marble stain easily?

Marble is a natural stone and can absorb liquids if left unprotected. We recommend wiping spills promptly and using coasters or placemats. All of our marble products are professionally sealed to help resist stains and moisture.

Do you offer custom marble table sizes and designs?

Yes. We provide custom dimensions, marble selections, edge profiles, and base designs tailored to your interior style and space requirements. Contact us with your measurements and ideas for a personalized quotation.

Is every marble slab unique?

Absolutely. Natural marble features distinctive veining, textures, and color variations formed over millions of years. No two marble tables are exactly alike, making every piece truly one of a kind.
