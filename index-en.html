Frequently Asked Questions


Are marble tables durable for everyday use?

Yes. High-quality marble tables are extremely durable and can last for decades when properly maintained. Marble is naturally heat-resistant and strong, making it suitable for dining tables, coffee tables, and luxury interior spaces.

Does marble stain easily?

Marble is a natural stone and can absorb liquids if left unsealed. We recommend wiping spills promptly and using coasters or placemats. All of our marble products are professionally sealed to help resist stains and moisture.

Do you offer custom marble table sizes and designs?

Yes. We offer custom sizing, marble selections, edge profiles, and base designs to fit your interior style and space requirements. You can contact us with your preferred dimensions and design ideas for a personalized quotation.

Is every marble piece unique?

Absolutely. Natural marble features unique veining, textures, and color variations formed over thousands of years. No two marble tables are exactly the same, making each piece one of a kind.
